# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Life Happens handles information when the account owner connects
the local macOS application to Oura. Life Happens is intended for private, single-user use.

## Information accessed from Oura

With the owner's explicit authorization, Life Happens may access the Oura data needed for its
wellness dashboards, including:

- daily sleep, readiness, activity, stress, resilience, and cardiovascular summaries;
- sleep sessions and sleep-stage information;
- workouts, steps, calories, and other movement information;
- resting and intraday heart-rate information, including HRV where available;
- temperature and blood-oxygen information; and
- identifiers, dates, timestamps, time-zone offsets, units, and contributor values needed to
  organize and display those measurements accurately.

The exact information available depends on the permissions granted by the owner, the owner's Oura
membership, and the data available in the Oura account.

## How information is used

Oura information is used only to import, organize, calculate, and display the owner's personal
wellness history inside Life Happens. Life Happens does not use Oura information for advertising,
sell it, rent it, or send directly imported Oura API data to an artificial-intelligence model.

Life Happens does not make changes to the owner's Oura account. It performs Oura network requests
only during authorization, token maintenance required for an authorized request, revocation, or
when the owner explicitly selects **Sync now**.

## Storage and security

OAuth client credentials and Oura access and refresh tokens are stored locally in macOS Keychain.
They are not intentionally stored in the Life Happens database, application logs, URLs, or source
code.

Imported measurements are stored locally on the owner's Mac in the Life Happens application-data
directory. Life Happens does not operate a server that receives or stores the owner's imported Oura
measurements. Local information is protected by the security of the owner's macOS account and any
device protections the owner enables, such as FileVault and a strong login password.

No method of storage is completely secure. The owner is responsible for controlling access to the
Mac and keeping macOS reasonably up to date.

## Sharing and disclosure

Life Happens does not sell or share Oura information with advertisers, data brokers, or other
commercial third parties. Information may leave the Mac only through communications necessary to
use Oura's authorization and API services, or when the owner independently exports, copies, backs
up, or shares local application data.

Oura's own processing is governed by Oura's policies and the choices associated with the owner's
Oura account.

## Retention, disconnection, and deletion

Imported Oura information remains on the Mac until the owner deletes it or removes the application's
local data. Disconnecting Oura deletes the locally stored authorization credentials and, where
available, requests revocation from Oura; it does not automatically delete measurements previously
imported into Life Happens.

The owner can use the separate Oura-data deletion control in Life Happens to permanently delete
imported Oura records while retaining the authorization. The owner can also revoke Life Happens
from Oura's account or developer controls. Copies created independently through device backups or
manual exports may remain until the owner deletes those copies or their backup-retention period
expires.

## Children's privacy

Life Happens is not directed to children and is not intended to collect information from anyone who
is not permitted to authorize an Oura account under Oura's applicable terms.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if Life Happens changes how it handles information. The current version
will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
